8 · Phone view

What I found on the phone screenshots: each link takes almost a whole screen (a label on every line), the filters are uneven widths, and on the Dashboard a long link runs into the next box. New: a link is a short card: name and SKU, supplier and page, then its status and stock as two coloured words, then the buttons. Filters fold into one Filters button with a count, and the chosen ones show as chips. Dashboard links are short ("See suppliers"). Suppliers get the same short cards.
