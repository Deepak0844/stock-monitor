Check history in words only (C and D)

No strips anywhere. Healthy suppliers and links say "Last 24 checks fine"; a broken one says what went wrong and since when, in red, such as "3 failed from 07:48, then checks stopped". Use the switches to choose a direction, a page and a mode. Every screen uses the same sample suppliers and products. Suppliers and Links keep everything the real pages have (filters, search, status words, actions); only the look changes. Press "Pick this" on the direction you like, or tell me what to mix.
